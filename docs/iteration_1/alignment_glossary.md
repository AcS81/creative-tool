# Alignment Glossary: Redundant vs Complementary vs Conflicting

Quick reference for reading multimodal alignment notes and diagnostics.

- **Redundant** – Audio and visuals convey the same thing without adding meaning.  
  Example: saying “Here’s a graph of revenue” while showing the exact chart with no extra highlight or motion.
- **Complementary** – Each mode adds new, non-overlapping information.  
  Example: explaining “our speed doubled” while an on-screen chart shows the exact before/after numbers and a caption adds the precise delta.
- **Conflicting** – Modes clash or mislead.  
  Example: voice says “the launch was smooth” while the b-roll shows obvious glitches, or captions contradict spoken numbers.

Interpretation: complementary moments generally score highest; redundant moments are neutral unless overused; conflicting moments drag alignment and balance down.
